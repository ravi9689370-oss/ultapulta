# Privacy Policy — UltaPulta

Last updated: 2026-10-06

UltaPulta ("the App") is a single-player offline arcade game. We respect your
privacy and keep data collection to zero.

## What we collect
Nothing. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Local storage
The App stores only your best score and mute preference **on your device**
(localStorage / WebView local data). This never leaves your device and can be
erased by clearing the App's data.

## Permissions
The App requests no runtime permissions. No location, contacts, camera,
microphone, files, or network access is used for gameplay.

## Third-party services
None. The App contains no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting
SDKs, or third-party network calls.

## Children
The App is suitable for all ages and is directed at no specific age group.
No data is collected from children or adults.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL.
