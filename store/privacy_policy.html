Privacy Policy — UltaPulta

Last updated: 2026-10-06

UltaPulta is a single-player offline arcade game. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to zero.

What we collect

Nothing. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Local storage

The App stores only your best score and mute preference on your device. This never leaves your device and can be erased by clearing the App's data.

Permissions

The App requests no runtime permissions. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or network access is used for gameplay.

Third-party services

None. No analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party network calls.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages.
