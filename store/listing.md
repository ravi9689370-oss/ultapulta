## Full description (max 4000 chars)
UltaPulta is a fast, one-thumb arcade runner with a twist no other runner has:
the world flips. Without warning the screen mirrors itself and your controls
do the opposite of what you expect. Survive the mirror zones, read the
flickering glitch walls, and chase a score your friends can't beat.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a lane to dash to it.
• Walls scroll toward you — always reach the gap.
• When "MIRROR INCOMING" flashes, the screen will horizontally flip:
  tap the lane that truly matches, not the one that looks right.
• Mirror mode scores DOUBLE points.
• Glitchy dashed walls are fake — pass straight through them.

FEATURES
• One-thumb, tap-to-play controls
• Endless, ever-faster difficulty ramp
• Mirror-flip reality mechanic
• Offline play — no internet needed
• Haptics + sound effects (mutable)
• Local best score — no account, no ads, no tracking

Perfect for short breaks, queues, and 2 AM "one more try" sessions.
